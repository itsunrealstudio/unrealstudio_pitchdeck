Estimated timeline

Timelines depend on project scope, feedback turnaround and the timely sharing of content and assets.

  1. 01

    Discovery Call

    We understand your business, goals, audience and vision.

    You bring the idea. We’ll ask the right questions.
  2. 02

    Proposal & Scope

    We align on deliverables, pricing, timelines and expectations.

    You approve the plan before we begin.
  3. 03

    Kickoff & Direction

    We gather your content, brand assets and references to define the website’s direction.

    You share the essentials. We shape the strategy.
  4. 04

    Design & Approval

    We develop the visual direction and key page designs for your review.

    You share feedback. We refine and finalise.
  5. 05

    Development & Review

    We build the website, optimise it across devices and test its functionality.

    You review progress and provide consolidated feedback.
  6. 06

    Launch & Handover

    We complete final checks, take your website live and hand over the agreed assets and access.

    You approve the final version. We get you launch-ready.
